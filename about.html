About Lucky Fish

What is Lucky Fish?

Lucky Fish helps students and gamers on a budget find out what a PC game normally costs, what it costs right now and whether it is worth waiting for a better deal. We show prices from Steam, GOG and Epic Games in one place, so you don't have to check three stores yourself.

The name is a little joke - you are lucky to catch a discount and you catch a deal the way you catch a fish.

How prices work

Every game has a normal price (the usual price with no discount) and a sale price (what the store is charging right now). The discount tag on each cover shows how much cheaper the sale price is.

On a game's page you also see the lowest price seen. This is the cheapest price that CheapShark has ever recorded for that game at any store it tracks and the date it happened. With this, you can plan to wait for games that are known to have deals, if it was recently on sale at its lowest price.

Rand prices are estimates

The stores list their prices in US dollars. Lucky Fish converts them to South African Rand using one fixed exchange rate, which is R16.39 for US$1 (rate from 28 September 2026).

The rate does not update and the store may charge a different amount in Rand because of its own rates, fees or taxes. Use the flag button in the top corner to switch to US dollars and see the exact listed price.

Privacy (POPIA)

Lucky Fish has no accounts and no login. We don't ask for your name, email or any personal details.

Your Wishlist, Playing and Played shelves and your Rand or US dollar choice are saved in your own browser only (using localStorage). If you clear your browser data, your library is deleted.

Disclaimer

Lucky Fish is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with any store or game publisher. Prices change often and may be out of date by the time you reach the store, so always check the price on the store's own page before you buy.
